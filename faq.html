FOR BUSINESSES

Hiring and workforce support

What recruitment services does Meridian provide?＋

Meridian supports blue-collar and white-collar hiring, including requirement understanding, candidate sourcing, first-level screening and interview coordination. Share your role, location, headcount and target timeline through the hiring enquiry form.

Can Meridian support contract staffing and payroll?＋

Yes. Meridian lists contract staffing, payroll management and HR operations among its services. The scope, locations and process are discussed for each business requirement. See the services page.

Where does Meridian work?＋

Meridian is based in Delhi NCR and serves businesses across India. Share your work locations so the team can discuss the requirement.

What should I include in a hiring enquiry?＋

Useful starting details are role titles, approximate number of hires, work location, employment type and desired start timeline. Do not include candidate records or sensitive employee information in the initial enquiry.

FOR CANDIDATES

Applications and opportunities

Where can I find current openings?＋

Openings confirmed by Meridian appear on the Careers page. You can also send a general application for future opportunities; it is not an application to a specific vacancy.

How do I apply for a listed role?＋

Open the role and select its Apply button. The application form sends details to Meridian through Formspree. Resume upload is optional and depends on file uploads being enabled for the form.

What personal details should I send?＋

Send only the information needed to apply, such as your contact details, current city, experience and an optional resume. Do not send passwords, OTPs, bank details or identity document numbers through an initial application.

How can I check if a hiring message is from Meridian?＋

Use the contact details on this official website to confirm an opportunity. Do not share passwords, OTPs, bank information or money with an unverified person claiming to recruit for Meridian.
