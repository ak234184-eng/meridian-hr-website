Home/Insights/Payroll checklist

Adapt this checklist to your internal controls, organisation and applicable requirements. Use approved, secure channels for employee information.

1. Set an input cut-off

Publish dates for attendance, leave, overtime and variable-pay submissions. Confirm who approves each input and how late changes are handled.

2. Reconcile employee changes

Review approved joiners, exits, transfers, salary revisions and bank-detail changes against authorised HR records. Use approved secure channels for personal and banking data; do not place sensitive details in public forms or ordinary email threads.

3. Validate time and earnings inputs

Check attendance totals, leave, overtime and one-time earnings against source records and the approval trail. Investigate missing or unusual entries before processing.

4. Review deductions and statutory items

Check authorised deductions and recurring statutory inputs relevant to your organisation and location. Rules can change and vary, so confirm current treatment with a qualified payroll or compliance professional.

5. Review outputs and protect records

Use a second-person review where your process allows. Reconcile payroll totals to approved records, distribute payslips through the appropriate channel, restrict access to payroll files and retain records according to company policy and applicable requirements.
