YOUR INFORMATION

A clear note on
privacy and applications.

This notice describes what happens when you use Meridian HR & Staffing’s public website.

Home/Privacy
IN SHORT

You choose what to send.

This static website does not collect or store contact form submissions, job applications, or resumes.

Questions? Email Meridian ↗

Information you enter

The contact and application forms prepare a draft in your own email application. Nothing is sent when you press the form button. You can review, change, or discard the draft. Information is sent to Meridian only if you choose to send that email.

When applying, attach your resume yourself in the email draft. The website does not upload or store it. Please do not send passwords, bank details, or identity document numbers with an initial application.

Public job information

Open job titles and role descriptions are public on this site and in its public GitHub repository. Proposed listing changes in pull requests may also be visible before they are merged. Do not add candidate details, private contact data, or internal hiring notes to a listing.

Hosting and administrator sign in

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard request logs under its own privacy terms. To publish a job update, an authorized administrator signs in to GitHub; GitHub handles that sign-in and repository permissions.

WhatsApp and email

If you follow a WhatsApp link or send an email, the relevant provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Review your message before sending.

Contact and requests

For questions about an enquiry or application you sent to Meridian, email admin@meridian-hr.in. Meridian will handle that correspondence in its email system and can explain its retention practices on request.

Updates to this notice

If the site’s forms or data handling change, this notice will be updated to describe the new process.
